Chapter II — Obligations

DPDPA Section 6 — Consent

Applies to: Data Fiduciary / Consent Manager

What this section does

Consent must be free, specific, informed, unconditional and unambiguous, given by a clear affirmative action, and limited to the data necessary for the stated purpose. It must be as easy to withdraw as to give.

Plain-English summary prepared by KensaraAI — not the statutory text. Read the official Act ↗

What you have to do

  • ▸ No bundled or pre-ticked consent
  • ▸ Provide a withdrawal mechanism of equal ease
  • ▸ Stop processing and erase on withdrawal, absent another legal basis
