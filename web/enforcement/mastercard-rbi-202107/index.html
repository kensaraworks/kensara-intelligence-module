Mastercard — Reserve Bank of India

Data localisation non-compliance · 2021-07-14

What happened

The RBI barred Mastercard from onboarding new domestic customers for failing to comply with the 2018 payment-data localisation directive. The ban was lifted in June 2022 after compliance.
