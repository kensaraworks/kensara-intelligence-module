Key Takeaways for Indian Companies

1
DPDPA enforcement hasn't started yet — but it will
The Data Protection Board of India is not yet fully operational. When it is, the ₹250 crore per violation penalty will be live. Start compliance now.
2
CERT-In's 6-hour rule is active and enforced now
In force since June 2022. Failure to report within 6 hours carries penalty. Automate breach detection.
3
Data localisation is India's most effective enforcement tool
RBI's business bans on Mastercard and Amex show India will use operational bans, not just fines.
4
Healthcare & Fintech face dual regulatory pressure
Obligations from multiple regulators simultaneously. A single platform covering all is essential.
